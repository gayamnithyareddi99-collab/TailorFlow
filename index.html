How It Works

From Checkout Intent to Ready-to-Wear Delivery in 24–48 Hours

Every TailorFlow deployment follows a four-stage fit orchestration journey from checkout capture and semantic translation, to pre-dispatch modification at a vetted micro-hub, to final "Ready-to-Wear" delivery all without disrupting the merchant's existing e-commerce stack or adding friction to the checkout funnel.

🛒
STEP 01

Capture at Checkout

The Checkout-to-Stitch Orchestration Layer (CSOL) captures customer fit preferences directly within Shopify or WooCommerce checkout hemming, waist taper, sleeve shortening as structured order metadata.

🤖
STEP 02

Translate & Standardise

The AITE Semantic Engine normalises vague customer language like "shorten for a petite fit" into precise technical job sheets e.g., "Hem: -4.5cm; blind stitch finish" with 95% mapping accuracy.

⚙️
STEP 03

Route & Alter

The Pre-Dispatch Modification Buffer intercepts the garment between "Pick" and "Dispatch" stages, routing it to the nearest certified tailoring micro-hub for completion within the SLA window.

✅
STEP 04

Verify & Deliver

Digital QA evidence is captured pre/post modification. The garment is dispatched as a "Ready-to-Wear" item while Fit Intelligence data feeds back to the merchant's design team to prevent future returns.

Core Platform

Five Proprietary Modules. One Fit Orchestration Infrastructure.

TailorFlow combines five proprietary orchestration modules into a single fashion-operations middleware the only platform that unifies Checkout Capture, Semantic Translation, Pre-Dispatch Routing, Digital QA, and Fit Intelligence into one proactive "Return Prevention" system for D2C brands, multicultural retailers, and luxury boutiques.

🧠
Module 01

CSOL Checkout Engine

Transaction-integrated framework that captures fit adjustments at the point of purchase, transforming customer intent into structured tailoring instructions in sub-200ms.

Unlike separate booking funnels, CSOL embeds the modification trigger directly within the merchant's ordering logic maintaining checkout conversion rates while capturing every alteration request with 100% technical fidelity.
🔤
Module 02

AITE Semantic Engine

Machine learning layer that converts ambiguous customer language into quantified, workshop-compatible parameters with 95% accuracy across 10+ garment categories.

The engine understands garment construction rules automatically validating requests to ensure they don't interfere with structural elements like zippers or vents, eliminating rework and craftsmanship disputes.
⚡
Module 03

Pre-Dispatch Buffer

Upstream intervention model that intercepts garments between "Pick" and "Dispatch" stages, routing them to certified micro-hubs before first-mile delivery.

The Modification Buffer is designed for high-volume retail ensuring compliance with e-commerce speed expectations (24–48h SLA) while preventing the 4.2kg of CO₂ generated by redundant return trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does TailorFlow integrate with my Shopify store?

+
TailorFlow integrates via a native Shopify Checkout UI Extension no custom code required. The extension is installed in under 30 minutes and presents product-specific alteration options based on SKU attributes, capturing structured alteration metadata without affecting your checkout conversion rate or sub-second page load benchmarks.

What is the 24–48 hour SLA and how is it enforced?

+
The SLA is enforced through real-time capacity monitoring and automated job redirection. If a tailoring hub approaches its throughput limit, jobs are automatically rerouted to the next nearest certified partner. Partner contracts include SLA penalty clauses, and TailorFlow's dashboard provides real-time milestone tracking for both merchants and end customers.

How does TailorFlow help with EPR 2025 compliance?

+
TailorFlow provides merchants with audited primary data on returns reduction and landfill prevention directly supporting reporting requirements under the UK Textiles Pact and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) scheme. Every modification is logged as a digital event, ready for export into your sustainability disclosure reports.

What types of garments and alterations are supported?

+
TailorFlow currently supports hemming, waist adjustments, tapering, and sleeve shortening across 10+ garment categories including trousers, occasionwear, ethnic wear (Kurta/Kameez), formalwear, and denim. Specialist modules for multicultural wear, bridal, and suiting include domain-specific tailoring logic and QA protocols.
